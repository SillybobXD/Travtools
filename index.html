Village planner

Plan building upgrades and troop training together, then see whether to trade with the NPC merchant or send resources from another village.

Village

Import from the game

In the game, open Village overview, then the Resources tab for resources or the Capacity tab for warehouse and granary. Select all, copy, and paste it here. You can paste one tab, then the other.

Building upgrades

Use “How many” for several buildings of the same kind, for example 6 croplands going from level 5 to 7.

Celebrations

Small celebrations need town hall level 1, great ones level 10. Costs are the standard values and haven’t been checked against the game yet.

Troops

Each troop type has its own way to plan: as many as possible, a set number, or enough to fill a training time. Set numbers and timed troops are paid first; “Max possible” types share the rest by their ratio, for example 2 and 1 for twice as many of the first.
