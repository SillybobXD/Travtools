In the game, open Village overview, then Culture points. Select all, copy, and paste it here. Then do the same with the Culture points tab of your Residence or Palace. Each paste adds what it has.

Celebrations and Artworks

A celebration's culture points come when it ends: small 500, at most the village's CP per day; great 2,000, at most the account's. An Artwork gives one day of the account's CP by default. Blank CP uses these defaults; neither rule is checked against the game yet.
